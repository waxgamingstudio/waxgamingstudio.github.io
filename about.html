About Wax Gaming

Creating fun, accessible, and enjoyable mobile games for players around the world.

🎮 Who We Are

Wax Gaming is an independent mobile game development brand focused on creating entertaining and accessible games for Android and iOS.

Wax Gaming is a developer brand operated by independent developer Waquar Ahmad. The name "Wax Gaming" is used as a brand identity for publishing and presenting games, applications, and related digital products created and maintained by Waquar Ahmad.

We believe games should be simple to play, enjoyable for everyone, and continuously improved based on player feedback.

👨‍💻 Developer & Brand Ownership

Waquar Ahmad is the individual developer and owner of the Wax Gaming brand. Wax Gaming and Waquar Ahmad refer to the same developer and owner.

Depending on the platform and account configuration, our applications may be published under:

  • Waquar Ahmad — individual developer identity
  • Wax Gaming — developer brand and publishing identity

These names represent the same individual developer and are not separate or unrelated developers. All games, applications, website content, support services, and published digital products under the Wax Gaming brand are created, owned, and maintained by Waquar Ahmad.

📱 Cross-Platform Publishing

Wax Gaming publishes and distributes games across multiple platforms.

Our games may appear under the Wax Gaming brand on platforms such as Google Play, while applications submitted through an individual developer account may display the legal or account-holder name Waquar Ahmad.

This difference in displayed developer name is due to the platform account configuration and does not indicate that the applications are owned or developed by different companies or individuals.
