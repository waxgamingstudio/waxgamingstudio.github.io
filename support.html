❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The game won't start or crashes. What should I do?
A: Try restarting your device and ensure you have the latest version of the game installed. If the issue continues, contact us with your device model and operating system version.

Q: I found a bug. How can I report it?
A: Send us an email describing the issue and, if possible, include screenshots or a screen recording to help us investigate.

Q: Will my game progress be saved?
A: Progress is typically stored locally on your device. Removing the app may result in the loss of saved data unless cloud saving is specifically supported.

Q: How can I share feedback or suggestions?
A: We love hearing from players. Feel free to email us with any ideas, suggestions, or feedback about our games.

Q: How long does it take to receive a response?
A: We aim to respond to all support requests within 3–5 business days.
